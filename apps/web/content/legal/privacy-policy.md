---
title: Privacy Policy
---

This repository does not define the privacy policy for your deployment.

Before accepting interview data, the operator must replace this page with a
policy that identifies the operator and explains:

- which account, audio, transcript, translation, message, note, and log data is
  collected;
- the purposes and legal basis for processing;
- retention, deletion, export, and participant-request procedures;
- the ASR, translation, AI, hosting, mail, analytics, and storage providers that
  receive data, including their processing regions;
- security contacts and applicable user rights.

Do not use an unconfigured deployment for real interviews or personal data.
