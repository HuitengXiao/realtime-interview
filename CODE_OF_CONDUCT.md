# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.
We expect participants to communicate professionally, assume good intent, and
respect differing experiences and viewpoints.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive and considerate in issues, pull requests, and reviews.
- Focus feedback on ideas and implementation, not people.
- Respect privacy: do not share credentials, recordings, transcripts, or real
  interview participant information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate
disclosure of private information, and any other conduct that makes the
community unsafe or unwelcoming are not tolerated.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's maintainer contact.
Maintainers may remove content, issue warnings, or restrict participation as
needed to protect the community. Retaliation against someone who raises a
concern is not tolerated.
